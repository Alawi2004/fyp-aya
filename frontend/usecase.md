System Users
2.1 Driver
The driver uses the frontend to manage trips, verify passengers, track
earnings, and monitor routes in real time.
1. View Bus Name, Schedule, and Trip Time
The driver can see the assigned bus name, trip time, and route
details.
Purpose: This ensures the driver is aware of their schedule and avoids
confusion between different buses or routes.
2. Notifications for Upcoming Trips
The system sends reminders before each trip starts.
Purpose: Helps the driver prepare on time and improves punctuality.
3. QR Code Scanning for Passenger Verification
The driver scans the passenger’s QR code when boarding.
Purpose:
Confirms the passenger has a valid ticket.
Prevents the use of expired or fake tickets.
Automatically deducts the fare from the passenger’s account.
Updates the driver’s earnings instantly.
4. View Number of Passengers (Boarded / Missing)
The driver can see how many passengers are expected and how
many have boarded.
Purpose:
Ensures no registered passenger is left behind.
Helps manage seating and capacity.
Improves trip organization and safety.
5. GPS Tracking and Route Monitoring
The driver can view the route map and current trip tracking.
Purpose:
Ensures the driver follows the correct route.
Provides navigation assistance.
Keeps the system updated with live location for passengers.
6. Trip History and GPS History
The driver can view completed trips and route history.
Purpose:
Allows performance tracking.
Useful for reports and system monitoring.
7. Earnings Dashboard
The driver can see:
Earnings per trip
Daily/weekly/monthly earnings
Purpose:
Provides financial transparency.
Motivates drivers by showing performance.
Eliminates manual payment calculations.
8. Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA)
The driver can see the estimated time of arrival to the next stop and
final destination.
Purpose:
Helps the driver stay aligned with the schedule.
Allows better time management during traffic.
Reduces delays and improves punctuality.
Increases service reliability.
9. Delay Reporting / Issue Reporting
The driver can report issues such as:
Traffic congestion
Vehicle problems
Passenger incidents
Route blockages
Purpose:
Keeps the system updated in real time.
Automatically notifies passengers about delays.
Improves communication between driver and management.
Enhances passenger trust and safety.
10. Emergency Alert Button
The driver has access to an emergency alert feature in case of
accidents or serious situations.
Purpose:
Immediately notifies system administrators.
Ensures passenger safety.
Provides quick response support.
Adds a layer of security to the system.
11. Break / Trip Status Control
The driver can update trip status:
Trip Started
In Progress
Completed
On Break
Purpose:
Keeps passengers informed.
Improves trip tracking accuracy.
Ensures real-time system synchronization.
2.2 Passenger
The passenger frontend focuses on convenience, real-time updates,
and secure ticket management.
1. View Bus Schedule and Availability
Passengers can see available buses, times, and seat availability.
Purpose:
Helps plan travel in advance.
Reduces waiting time at bus stops.
2. GPS Tracking and Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA)
Passengers can see the live location of the bus and its estimated
arrival time.
Purpose:
Reduces uncertainty.
Helps passengers arrive at the stop at the correct time.
3. Notifications (Arriving / Delayed)
Passengers receive notifications if:
The bus is arriving soon.
The bus is delayed.
Purpose:
Keeps passengers informed.
Improves customer satisfaction.
Reduces frustration caused by delays.
4. Digital Ticket Information
Passengers can view their ticket details including:
Bus name
Seat number (example: A12)
Date (e.g., 2025-01-28)
Time (e.g., 10:30 AM)
Price
Purpose:
Ensures the ticket is valid and not expired.
Prevents misuse of old tickets.
Confirms correct seat allocation.
5. QR Code for Boarding
Passengers press a button to display their QR code.
The driver scans it when boarding.
Purpose:
Fast boarding process.
Secure payment confirmation.
Eliminates paper tickets and cash handling.
6. Rating and Feedback System
Passengers can rate the trip and leave comments.
Purpose:
Allows service improvement.
Provides accountability for drivers.
Helps maintain high service quality.
7. Special Offers and Loyalty Rewards
Passengers who frequently use the service can receive special offers
such as:
Discounted tickets
Free trips after a certain number of rides
Promotional coupons
How it Works:
The system tracks how often a passenger travels. After reaching a
specific number of trips (for example, 10 trips), the passenger
automatically receives a reward or discount notification.
Purpose:
Encourages customer loyalty.
Increases long-term usage of the system.
Rewards regular passengers.
8. Trip History
Passengers can view their completed trips including:
Bus name
Date
Time
Seat number
Payment amount
Trip status
Purpose:
Provides payment transparency.
Helps passengers track their travel history.
Supports loyalty reward tracking.
Useful for issue reporting.
9. Cancel or Modify Booking
Passengers can cancel or reschedule a trip before departure (based
on system policy).
Purpose:
Adds flexibility for passengers.
Reduces no-shows.
Improves user satisfaction.
10. Profile and Payment Management
Passengers can:
Update personal information.
Add or remove payment methods.
View account balance.
Purpose:
Ensures secure and updated information.
Makes payments smooth and automatic.
Improves user control over their account.
11. Seat Selection Feature
Passengers can choose their seat (example: A12) when booking.
Purpose:
Prevents seat conflicts.
Improves comfort and organization.
Gives passengers more control over their travel experience.
12. Favorite Routes / Quick Booking
Passengers can save frequently used routes.
Purpose:
Speeds up booking process.
Encourages frequent usage.
Enhances convenience for daily commuters
